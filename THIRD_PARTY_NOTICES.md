## MathJax

The pages PlotlySupply renders (the desktop window, the hidden export window and
exported HTML files) load MathJax 2.7.9 from the cdnjs CDN
(`https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/mathjax/2.7.9/`) to typeset `$...$`
labels. MathJax is not bundled with PlotlySupply.
